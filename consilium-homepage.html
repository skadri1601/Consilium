How it works

Six phases.
One running example.

Every Council deliberation moves through these phases. Click through to see what each looks like with a real question: "Monolith or microservices at 50k DAU?"

Each model analyzes the question in isolation and commits to a position with reasoning. No model sees another's answer yet.
GPT-4o · Modular monolith
At 50k DAU, operational risk of K8s exceeds scaling benefit. Keep it boring.
Claude 4.5 · Modular monolith
Extract notifications and billing as first split candidates. Not yet.
Gemini 2.0 · Microservices now
Team already knows K8s. A second migration later will cost more than starting distributed today.
Models read each other's positions and file specific challenges against the reasoning, not the conclusion.
Gemini → GPT-4o
The 50k DAU threshold is arbitrary. Where's the load-pattern evidence?
Claude → Gemini
"Migration cost later" ignores the blast radius of a bad split now.
GPT-4o → Gemini
"Team has K8s experience" doesn't equal "team can run K8s in prod under a 99.9% SLA."
Each model responds to challenges: concede, refute, qualify, or redirect. Positions get revised in view of the exchange.
GPT-4o · qualifies
Fair. The threshold is a heuristic. At this traffic, coordination cost of microservices dominates any scaling gain.
Gemini · concedes partially
Granted on the SLA risk. Still argue the migrate-later cost is underweighted.
Claude · refutes
Bounded contexts inside a monolith are reversible. Premature microservices are not.
A judge model scores each surviving position on a weighted rubric. No model scores itself.

Deliberation modes

Start with Council.
Reach for the rest when you need them.

Eight modes, one default. The others exist for specific jobs: speed, anonymity, adversarial probing, or consensus mechanics.

Default mode

Council.

Multi-round deliberation between models with cross-examination and rebuttal. Use this unless the task clearly calls for one of the specialized modes below.

3 rounds · ~45s · $0.05–0.10 per run
Try Council ↗
Speed vs depth

Quick

~15s

Single round. When you need a fast sanity check, not a debate.

Deep

~90s

Five rounds with sub-agent research. High-stakes or ambiguous questions.

Evaluation & safety

Blind

~45s

Model names hidden until scoring. Removes brand anchoring from the verdict.

Red Team

~120s

Models actively attack each other's arguments. Security and robustness review.

Consensus mechanism

Jury

~60s

Panel voting with ranked choice. Reach consensus or declare dissent.

Market

~90s

Prediction market aggregation. Models stake confidence on positions.

Auto

Can't decide? Consilium picks the right mode based on topic complexity and risk.

SDK

Structured in.
Structured out.

Every verdict comes back as a typed object. Verdict, confidence, per-model votes, and dissent are fields, not free text you have to parse.

The callPython
from consilium import ConsiliumClient

client = ConsiliumClient(api_key="your-key")

result = client.deliberate(
    topic="Monolith or microservices
            at 50k DAU?",
    mode="council",
    models=[
        "gpt-4o",
        "claude-sonnet-4-5",
        "gemini-2.0-flash",
    ],
)
The resultREPL

Research

Not a reading list.
A spec.

Each paper below maps to a specific Consilium feature. The deliberation protocol implements peer-reviewed findings, not vibes.

Khan et al. - ICML 2024

Debating with More Persuasive LLMs Leads to More Truthful Answers

AI debate produces more truthful answers than single-model prompting, even when one debater argues for the wrong answer.

Powers Blind mode
Du et al. - ICML 2024

Improving Factuality and Reasoning via Multiagent Debate

Multi-agent debate significantly improves factual accuracy and mathematical reasoning across multiple benchmarks.

Powers Challenge & Rebut phases
Chen et al. - ACL 2024

ReConcile: Round-Table Reasoning via Consensus

Structured multi-round discussion with confidence-weighted voting outperforms single-model and simple ensemble approaches.

Powers Vote & confidence calibration
Irving et al. - OpenAI / Anthropic

AI Safety via Debate

Debate between AI systems provides a scalable mechanism where adversarial interaction surfaces deceptive or incorrect reasoning.
